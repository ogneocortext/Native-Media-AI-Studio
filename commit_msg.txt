Fix image sidecars: three broken links made all metadata unreachable

Reported as 'a lot of bizarre looking imagery in the media library'. The images
are promptless renders (a bathtub containing a second staircase, a disembodied
arm fused to a cushion) - and the reason they could not be explained is that
their sidecars could not be found.

THREE INDEPENDENT BREAKS IN ONE CHAIN

1. The lookup filename matched nothing. outputs.load_sidecar_metadata looked
   for <stem>.png.json; every writer produces <stem>.json (go-worker's
   sidecarPath does filepath.Join(outputDir, filename+".json") where filename
   is the stem). Measured: 0 files matching *.png.json against 38 real sidecars.

2. The worker wrote to the wrong directory. go-worker's outputDir is output/,
   not output/images/, so sidecars landed away from the image they describe.
   Since the lookup resolves the sidecar relative to the image, a sidecar in the
   wrong directory is as unreachable as one with the wrong name.

3. The returned path was dangling. image_generator returned
   output/images/<name>.json regardless of where the worker actually wrote, so
   every job result carried a sidecar_path that did not exist on disk.

FIXES
- load_sidecar_metadata accepts <stem>.json, keeping the old name as a fallback.
- Sidecar persistence extracted into _persist_sidecar, which verifies the
  worker's report and RELOCATES the sidecar beside its image per Guidelines
  3.3, falling back to a direct write if the worker claims success falsely.
  It returns where the file really is.
- 56 orphaned sidecars moved next to their images; 47 with no matching image
  were left alone rather than guessed at.

Verified live: /api/outputs went from 0 images carrying metadata to 56 of 84,
returning the full record (prompt, model, seed, steps, cfg, sampler).

Kept out of scope on purpose: this makes the images explicable, not good. 158
of 160 image_generation jobs carry empty params, so every sidecar reads
prompt:'' and model:'default'. Generating a coherent picture from an empty
prompt is not possible - that is what the gallery is showing. That class of job
is already known and instrumented (queue/manager.py:353-380 documents 233 such
rows, logs the origin, and dead-letters them), so I did not re-report it as a
new finding. What is missing is cleanup of the residue.

The nesting gate caught my own regression: the verification logic pushed
save_output from control-flow depth 2 to 4. Refactored instead of re-baselining
- the baseline exists to stop nesting growing, and rewriting it to hide my
change would defeat it. Now reports '3 improved'.

6 regression tests added; all 7 gates pass.
